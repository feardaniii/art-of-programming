# Barista Field Guide High-Level Checklist

Small additions and corrections that keep the guide's plain-language style. This is intentionally non-technical: it is for improving the reader's mental model, not for explaining the repo.

## Suggested Checklist

- [ ] Phase 1: replace "It doesn't summarize them yet - it just maps them" with "It maps and summarizes your uploads, but it has not written the course summary yet."
- [ ] Phase 1: define `DocumentMap` as a smart source map, not just a table of contents: what sections exist, what each section is about, what topics are searchable, and what the document seems to cover overall.
- [ ] Phase 1: keep the "scope blindness" line. It is one of the clearest explanations in the guide.
- [ ] Retrieval: add one plain sentence that the engine searches both by meaning and by exact words, so rare names, terms, and examples do not disappear.
- [ ] HITL wording: say "major review points pause for approval" instead of implying every single phase pauses.
- [ ] Phase 4: say the engine builds the course-specific parts of the 7-layer model. Avoid implying all seven layers are generated in the same way.
- [ ] Phase 4: keep assessments out of the bonus-agent explanation. Say assessments appear later through exercises, quizzes, lesson review, and content checks.
- [ ] Phase 5: keep "No chapter content yet." A good add-on phrase would be: "This is the floor plan, not the furniture."
- [ ] Phase 6: mention that a presentation style is chosen before each lesson is written, and presentation-specific output may be generated after the lesson is assembled.
- [ ] Phase 6: add that completed lessons can surface section by section, so review does not need to wait for the whole course.
